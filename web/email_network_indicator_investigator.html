Email & Network Indicator Investigator

Offline, defensive triage for synthetic email headers and supplied indicators. Parses evidence locally in this browser; it does not contact DNS, mail servers, or threat-intelligence services.

1. Email headers

Paste raw headers. Received fields are displayed in the order supplied; the tool does not assume they are trustworthy.

2. Supplied DNS / indicators

Paste records or indicators gathered separately. No lookups are performed.

Use reserved example domains and documentation IP ranges for practice. Do not paste confidential messages or real personal data.

Analyst notes and limitations

Method and interpretation

  • Header values are untrusted input. A sender can forge many visible fields; trusted receiving infrastructure and raw message context matter.
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC results are reported as written in supplied headers, not independently validated.
  • Received headers are normally prepended by receiving systems. The earliest visible hop is not automatically the true origin; trust boundaries must be established.
  • DNS records are only parsed from the supplied text. A missing record in this input does not mean the record does not exist.
  • ATT&CK references are behavioural context, not proof that an intrusion occurred.
